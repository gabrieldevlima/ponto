TABELAS NOVAS EM V2 (9)
=======================

1. teacher_trusted_devices
   - Rastreia aparelhos confiáveis por professor
   - Cols: id, teacher_id, device_fingerprint, device_token, label, enrolled_at, last_used_at, enrollment_method, is_active
   - UKs: uk_teacher_fp (teacher_id, device_fingerprint), uk_ttd_device_token (device_token)
   - Backfilled: ~140 apparelhos com 3+ check-ins em 30 dias

2. attendance_edits
   - Auditoria de cada edicao em attendance
   - Cols: id, attendance_id, edited_by, edited_at, reason, type, diff_minutes, changed_fields, before_json, after_json
   - FKs: attendance (CASCADE), admins (RESTRICT)

3. audit_logs
   - Log centralizado de acoes administrativas
   - Cols: id, admin_id, action, entity, entity_id, payload, ip, created_at
   - FK: admins (RESTRICT)
   - Exemplo: action=approve, entity=attendance, entity_id=1234

4. auth_attempt_logs
   - Log de tentativas de autenticacao
   - Cols: id, attempt_type, identifier, teacher_id, ip_address, user_agent, success, reason, details, created_at
   - FK: teachers (SET NULL)
   - Protege contra brute-force

5. liveness_nonces
   - Desafios unicos para vivacidade facial
   - Cols: id, teacher_id, nonce, created_at
   - Lifetime: 5 minutos (stale cleanup em checkin.php)

6. fraud_detection_log
   - Log de deteccoes de fraude
   - Cols: id, attendance_id, teacher_id, detection_type, risk_level, details, ip_address, user_agent, created_at
   - Tipos: gps_mock, behavioral_anomaly, timing_anomaly

7. class_periods
   - Grade horaria (periodos de aula)
   - Cols: id, school_id (NULL=global), period_number, start_time, end_time, active, created_at
   - UK: uq_school_period (school_id, period_number)
   - Dados: 10 periodos globais inseridos (7:00-7:50, 7:50-8:40, etc)

8. teacher_class_assignments
   - Atribui professor a periodos de aula
   - Cols: id, teacher_id, weekday (0-6), period_id, school_id, created_at
   - UK: uq_teacher_weekday_period (teacher_id, weekday, period_id)
   - Permite: professor X tem aula no periodo Y na terca-feira

9. permissions
   - Controle granular de acesso (tabela, ainda nao integrada)
   - Cols: id, admin_id, permission_key, granted_at
   - UK: uk_admin_perm (admin_id, permission_key)
