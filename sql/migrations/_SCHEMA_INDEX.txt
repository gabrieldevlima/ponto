TABELAS NOVAS (9)
=================

1. teacher_trusted_devices
   - FK: teachers.id
   - Indexes: uk_teacher_fp, uk_ttd_device_token, idx_teacher_active
   - Backfilled: ~140 devices com 3+ check-ins

2. attendance_edits
   - FK: attendance.id, admins.id
   - Audit de cada edicao

3. audit_logs
   - FK: admins.id
   - Log de acoes admin

4. auth_attempt_logs
   - FK: teachers.id (nullable)
   - Log de tentativas de auth

5. liveness_nonces
   - FK: teachers.id
   - Desafios unicos para vivacidade (5 min lifetime)

6. fraud_detection_log
   - FK: attendance.id, teachers.id
   - Log de fraudes detectadas

7. class_periods
   - FK: schools.id (nullable)
   - Grade horaria da instituicao
   - Data: 10 periodos globais inseridos

8. teacher_class_assignments
   - FK: teachers.id, class_periods.id, schools.id
   - Atribui professor a periodos

9. permissions
   - FK: admins.id
   - Controle granular de acesso (ainda nao integrado)
